# Machine repeat audit, 2026-11-21

Window: 2026-11-07 16:46Z to 2026-11-21 16:46Z (14 days), every engine we log.

- 381 posts, 81 distinct machines.
- By channel: Instagram Reels 80, Instagram Stories 79, Facebook Reels 70,
  Facebook Stories 70, YouTube Shorts 25, TikTok 20, LIST IT FREE Story 13,
  Just listed 12 on Instagram and 12 on Facebook.
- Real listings: 29 of 381 posts (8%). Just listed showed 12 distinct items (2 of

- Typefully / X: about 7 posts, mostly text. The generator showed twice (11-07, 11-10).
- HeyCatch: not in our logs; it picks its own content.

## Why
1. **One clip, six surfaces.** Each Reel also goes to Facebook, both Stories, TikTok
   and YouTube. One pick shows the machine about six times.
2. **Same opener photo under different slugs.** The 10-day rule was per slug and per
   platform, so these never counted as repeats:
   - forklift: 7 clips (commercial-idle-forklift, forklift-finally-deal,
     forklift-sitting-there, free-to-list, make-an-offer, message-the-seller,
     save-a-search)
   - compressor: 4 (ai-lists-it, snap-it-list-it, list-4-boosted-free,
     show-the-cold-start)
   - dozer: 3 (dealer-storefront, storefront-30-days-free, storefront-founding-29)
   - hydraulic cylinders: 3 (surplus-parts-labels, part-tag-scan, snap-the-part)
   - generator: 2 (import-from-your-website, job-wrapped-generator)
   - pairs: well pump, electric motor, parts shelf, combi oven, welder, boom lift,
     cargo trailer, white box truck, red pressure washer, tool wall
3. **The LIST IT FREE Story** rotated 7 fixed machines by weekday (each twice in 14
   days) from a 16-image bank.
4. **Forced picks.** `boost_until` pushes and the daily founding promo always used the
   same clips (the promo uses the dozer storefront clips).
5. **Real inventory barely used.** About 115 live listings, but only 12 reached a post.
   The live pool leans to excavators (36), parts (19) and fleet trucks (15), and one
   seller has 38 listings, so it needs one-per-seller and category spread.

## What changed (this PR)
- `machine` key on all 99 library clips; `rotation/rotation.py` merges every log and
  blocks a machine on all channels for 14 days after its 36-hour run.
- Reels, Stories, TikTok, List it free and promo picks follow it (`reels/post_reels.py`).
- The LIST IT FREE Story picks its machine by rotation (`stories/make_bg.py`).
- `listings/listing_reels.py` turns up to 6 live listings a day into 12-second Reels
  (new listings first, one per seller, categories mixed), held for the daily review
  unless the listing's card photo was already approved.

## Expected effect
Library only (74 machines, 13 free on 11-21), 9 Reels a day would post about 9 a day
for 4 days, then 4 to 8 a day, skipping about 16 slots a week. Listing Reels fill those
slots once the daily review approves them. Fewer posts on some days is the intended
trade for no repeats.
