# Reels and Stories to Instagram and Facebook, plus Threads and TikTok

Free, automatic Reels for @yardswaps (Instagram) and the YardSwaps Facebook Page, posted by GitHub Actions (free for this public repo) through Meta's own API. No scheduler subscription, no Zapier tasks, nothing on anyone's computer.

## What it does
- Ten Reels a day on each platform (two until 2026-11-08, then four, five once the library reached 50 videos, six from 2026-11-11 at 64 active, seven from 2026-11-13 at 71 active, eight from 2026-11-18 at 89 active, nine from 2026-11-20 at 96 active, and ten from 2026-11-23 at 110 active): 6:10 am, 7:00 am, 8:30 am, 10:00 am, 3:55 pm, 5:15 pm, 6:30 pm, 10:10 pm and 11:40 pm Central, plus 12:40 am, set in Central time (`SLOTS_CT` in `post_reels.py`), so daylight saving doesn't move them. HeyCatch posts at about :33 past 4 am, 12 pm and 8 pm Central (on Instagram and Facebook it also uses those same slots for its extra posts), and the Just listed photo post goes out at 2:15 pm, so each slot has a short window that keeps every post 90+ minutes clear of them: 6:10-6:55 am, 7:00-8:00 am, 8:30-9:30 am, 10:00-11:00 am, 3:55-4:45 pm, 5:15-6:00 pm, 6:30-7:00 pm, 10:10-11:00 pm, 11:40-11:59 pm, 12:40-1:15 am. Before posting, the script also checks the last posts on each account and skips that slot if anything that isn't ours went up in the last 90 minutes. `slots` in `library.json` (e.g. `["07:00-09:30", "10:00-11:00"]`) changes the slots without touching code; fewer slots also lowers the daily cap.
- Picks the next video from `library.json`: videos never posted on that platform first (newest `added` first, then `priority`). Once every video has run, repeats go best performer first: each video's past Instagram Reels are scored by follows x10, shares x4, saves x3, comments x3, likes x1 (follows, shares and saves need the `instagram_manage_insights` permission; without it the score uses likes and comments). An optional `boost` number on a library entry adds to its score, so the weekly batch can push a cut that brought app signups. The same video never goes out twice on a platform within 10 days. Above that sits the machine rotation (2026-11-21, `rotation/README.md`): a machine (the equipment a clip shows, the `machine` key in `library.json`) is blocked on every channel for 14 days after its 36-hour run, whichever clip shows it; with nothing free the slot takes the machine shown longest ago but never one from the last 7 days, else it skips. On Instagram a repeat goes out as a Trial Reel (shown to non-followers first, shared to followers only if it does well), so followers don't see reruns.
- Uploads the file from `library/` directly, builds the caption and writes the result to `posted.json` (time, platform, video, post id, link).
- Safety cap: at most one of our Reels per slot (4) per platform in any 20 hours.
- Stories: right after each Reel, the same video goes to the @yardswaps Instagram Story and the YardSwaps Page Story (one per Reel slot, each live 24 hours). A Story re-shares the newest Reel from the last 24 hours that hasn't been a Story yet, so a skipped Reel slot means no Story that slot. Stories don't count toward the Reel cap or the 90-minute spacing, and a failed Story never fails the run (it shows as a warning and `failed` in `posted.json`). The API can't add link stickers or captions to Stories, so the link lives in the bio and in the Reel caption. Same token and permissions as Reels (`instagram_content_publish`, `pages_manage_posts`).

## Captions (built by `post_reels.py` from each video's `body`, `question`, `ig_tags`)
Goal: engagement, follows and app downloads. Every caption has the body, a follow line, the app link, hashtags, and ends with the open question.
- Instagram: body; "Follow @yardswaps for a new one every day. Comment "list" and we'll message you the free listing link, or tap yardswaps.com/i in our bio." (the keyword fires the Meta comment-to-DM automation); up to 5 hashtags; the question.
- Facebook: body; "Follow the YardSwaps page for a new one every day. Get the free app and list it free: https://www.yardswaps.com/sell-equipment?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=reels_<slug>" (clickable, so it carries a per-Reel tag instead of the /f short link); a clip with `"category": "<id>"` in library.json (an id from the app's CATEGORIES, such as `generators`) links `/sell-equipment/<id>` instead; its 2 machine hashtags plus #YardSwaps and the whole broad set (or `fb_tags` if set); the question.
- Hashtags: Instagram allows at most 5 per post since Dec 2025. Each Reel keeps the 2 machine/trade tags from its `ig_tags` (#forklift #forkliftforsale), always gets #UsedEquipment, and rotates 2 of the broad tags, moving 2 along every post: the jobsite tags Meta's Page assistant suggested on Nov 1 (#Jobsite #Tradespeople #IndustrialEquipment #EquipmentMarketplace #VerifiedSeller) plus the TikTok search terms from Nov 5 (#BlueCollar #ConstructionLife #SmallBusiness). TikTok uses `tt_tags()` in post_reels.py: 2 machine tags + #BlueCollar + #UsedEquipment + 1 of #ConstructionLife #SmallBusiness #BuildingThings #Jobsite (`tt_tags` in library.json overrides). When a video fits a TikTok search phrase ("pressure washer", "electrician work", "building things", "construction life", "small business", "blue collar"), the `body` opens with that phrase word for word, because TikTok search reads caption text, not only hashtags (CAPTIONS.md, Nov 5). `broad_tags` in `library.json` overrides the set; `ig_tags_fixed: true` on a video uses its `ig_tags` as written. #VerifiedSeller is accurate: the app gives sellers a Verified seller badge after a phone check.
- Backup runs: GitHub can start a scheduled run hours late or drop it (the Nov 1 4:30 pm run never started; the Nov 2 morning runs started about 9 hours late), so the workflow has 18 crons a day spread over the winter and summer slot times. Each run starts with a `gate` job that checks out only `post_reels.py`, `library.json` and `posted.json` and runs the script with `CHECK_ONLY=1`; the posting job (full checkout with the videos) only runs when a slot window is open and that slot hasn't posted yet, so most runs end in seconds. Both jobs check out the branch head, not the commit the run was queued at, so a run that waited behind another sees its log. A run that lands outside every window does nothing, so a late run never posts next to HeyCatch, and each slot posts once at most. Since Nov 23 the crons fire every 10 minutes through every slot window (winter and summer times), because GitHub's timer here is unreliable (Nov 22-23: 7 of 26 crons ran, most 30 minutes to 4 hours late) and the Claude scheduled tasks that used to start each slot are refused by the agent safety check since the evening of Nov 22. The posting job judges the slot by the gate's time (`SLOT_AT`), because its full checkout takes 4-5 minutes; before Nov 23 that made the 3:55 pm slot's only reliable start (4:42 pm) land after the window closed, so that slot never posted from Nov 18 to Nov 22.
- One-off posts: running the workflow by hand with `slug` set posts that video right away. It is logged with `forced: true`, so it doesn't use up one of the daily slots, and the 90-minute HeyCatch gap ignores our own posts.
- Queue order (Nov 1): Meta's Page assistant says jobsite-problem shorts (the proximity search one) reach 300+ views, so app-tool cuts that solve a jobsite hassle (find-it-nearby, spare-part, save-a-search, message-the-seller, make-an-offer) alternate with the idle-gear cuts that got good views. A new cut with a later `added` date still goes first. Nov 2: eight small-item / category-range cuts (micrometers, condenser, pressure washer, air movers, boring bars, truck wheel, chop saw, anchor adhesive) are spread through that queue about one a day instead of running back to back; they share the Nov 1 `added` date so `priority` sets the order.
- Location tag: Instagram Reels are tagged with a place from `locations` in `library.json`, one per day: Houston 3 days of every 7 (`weight`: 3), then Dallas, El Paso, San Antonio and Austin. An entry gives a Facebook place Page `id`, or a `wikidata` id whose Facebook Places ID (P1997) is looked up at run time; a city with no id falls back to Houston. A dry run lists the rotation and asks Meta what each id is. If Meta refuses an id, the Reel posts without a location. Stories can't take a location or hashtags through the API.
- Rules (CAPTIONS.md, ENGAGEMENT-PLAYBOOK.md, UTM-LINKS.md in the campaign folder): seller-first, the body says "Free to list" and "Free boosts", plain words, no emoji or icons, one open question about the viewer's own gear ending in "?", never promise views or sales, posted as the business. No #smallbusiness, #fyp, #reels, #explore.

## Adding new videos (weekly batch)

Every clip ends on the YardSwaps end card (Alan, 2026-11-25; `brand/endcard/`). The posters
(`post_reels.py`, `post_tiktok.py`) only take entries marked `"endcard"`, which
`brand/endcard/endcard.py fix` sets after checking the last frames: a clip that already ends on the
blue card for 1 to 3 s is only marked; any other gets the card (2 s) in place of its old ending, the
storefront version for `"audience": "dealer"`. `listings/listing_reels.py` runs the fix every morning,
so a new clip waits at most a day; run it yourself after adding clips to post them sooner.
For each new 9:16 video with music:
1. Copy it to `reels/library/yardswaps-<slug>-9x16.mp4` (keep files under ~15 MB; Reels must be 3 to 60 seconds for Facebook).
2. Add an entry to `library.json`: `slug`, `file`, `priority` (0 = first), `added` (YYYY-MM-DD, today), `body` (seller text ending with a "Free to list ... Free boosts." line; a storefront clip ends on the storefront offer instead, worded as in `MESSAGING.md`, and never says listing is free), `question` (from the comment prompt bank, not used before, ending in "?"), `ig_tags`, optional `boost` (adds to the repeat score), optional `"audience": "dealer"` for a storefront clip (its Facebook caption then links yardswaps.com/dealers with "Open your storefront:" instead of the seller page, its Instagram and Threads captions point to yardswaps.com/s, and its TikTok caption closes on the storefront), `machine` (the equipment it shows; reuse the key of any clip cut from the same opener photo, a new photo gets a new key, see `rotation/README.md`), optional `hold: true` (+ `hold_reason`) to keep a video out of Reels, Stories and repeats until its cut is fixed. A fixed cut of an already-posted video goes in as a new slug with today's `added` date, so it posts at the next slot.
3. Commit to `main`. Newest `added` posts first.
Never delete `reels/` when clearing a week folder.

## TikTok
`.github/workflows/tiktok.yml` runs `post_tiktok.py`, which posts two a day, one in each Central-time window: 7:42-9:40 am and 2:42-4:40 pm (`TIKTOK_WINDOWS_CT`). Since Nov 23 the crons fire every 10 minutes through both windows in winter and summer time, and a cheap `gate` job (like the Reels one) lets only the first run in a window post; the Claude scheduled tasks that used to dispatch it are refused by the agent safety check since Nov 22. A run outside the windows posts nothing unless it forces a `slug`, all 90+ minutes clear of HeyCatch, which also posts to TikTok at about 4:30 am, 12:30 pm and 8:30 pm Central. If a manual run would land within 90 minutes of a HeyCatch slot, the script moves the Buffer publish time to 91 minutes after it. Same `library/`, same pick order, same 10-day no-repeat, at most 2 in 20 hours and never within 220 minutes of our last TikTok. Nov 10: cut from 5 a day to 3 after TikTok blocked a post as "potential spam" (about 8 a day with HeyCatch on a new account, every caption ending the same way), then to 2 after a second block that afternoon. Automatic back-off: each run reads the TikTok channel's failed posts from Buffer, marks them `failed` in the log with TikTok's reason, and if any failed as spam (`spam_risk`) in the last 72 hours it posts nothing (`SPAM_COOLDOWN_H`). Failed videos go back in the queue after that. Raise the daily count only after a clean week. `check` = 1 also lists the channel's recent sent and failed Buffer posts with the error text. Repeats are ranked by the video's Instagram engagement. If HeyCatch posts one of our library videos to TikTok, add `{"slug": "<slug>", "time": "<ISO time>"}` to `heycatch-tiktok.json` and it counts as posted for the 10-day rule. `"next_on": ["tiktok"]` on a library entry puts that clip first in line for the next scheduled TikTok slot (it replaces that slot's list-it-free or promotion post, inside the same daily cap and windows) until it has gone out once (Nov 25, the TikTok-safe storefront ad). Log: `tiktok-posted.json` (each entry goes `scheduled`, then `posted` with the TikTok link on the next run; `unconfirmed` after 3 hours means look in Buffer for a failed post).

Caption: body, with its closing free-listing paragraph ("Free to list...", "List it free:...", "No listing fee...") swapped for one of 4 `FREE_LINES` and one of 5 `CTA_LINES` in `post_tiktok.py` (they rotate at different rates, so a pair repeats only every 20 posts); TikTok's own 5 hashtags from `tt_tags()` in `post_reels.py` (2 machine tags, #BlueCollar, #UsedEquipment, 1 rotating; a `tt_tags` entry in `library.json` overrides); the question last. No web address, no "follow for" line and no @handle in TikTok captions: TikTok's spam filter reads repeated text, follow asks and links, and the link in the bio does the job.

Why Buffer and not TikTok's own API: TikTok lets an unaudited app post only private ("only me") videos from a private account, and its guidelines say the audit is not granted for "a utility tool to help upload contents to the account(s) you or your team manages" (https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines). Buffer is an audited TikTok partner and its free plan includes the API (1 key, 1,000 requests per channel per 30 days; this uses about 150) and 10 queued posts per channel that refill as they publish (this keeps 1 queued at a time). Buffer only takes a public link, so the video is served from this public repo through jsDelivr at the exact commit.
