A cinematic brand spot for organic Reels, Shorts, TikTok and YouTube. It is not a paid ad. Music only, no voice. It was made on the ElevenLabs startup grant with no spend.

- Scenes: Veo 3.1 at 1080p in ElevenLabs flow 2RUOCqBpsMvTX8rswJ5f, saved in `narration/brand-commercial/`. Three scenes came with letterbox bars and were cropped to full frame. The fryer shot uses its first 1.7 s slowed down, because someone walks through the frame later in the clip.
- Music: ElevenLabs Music v2.5 take B, instrumental, generated on our account. It builds and lifts at about 22 s, then is compressed and normalized to -14 LUFS.
- Rules checked: idle gear only, nothing running or driving. The real washer is the same item in its scene and in the app. No faces. No real app screen redrawn. No AI label on the video, so turn on each platform's AI-content toggle when posting.
- Rebuild: `source/build.sh <clip dir> <music> 0` (clip prep commands are in the README history).
