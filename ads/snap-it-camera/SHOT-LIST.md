# Shot list: the truly live "Snap it" ad (10 minutes at a yard)

Goal: real footage of a real hand taking a real photo of a real machine, then the real app listing that same photo.
Nothing generated. This replaces the camera-screen opener in `yardswaps-snap-it-camera-*.mp4` with your clips;
the rest of the ad (captions, end card, music) stays the same.

## What you need
- Two phones: **Phone A** films, **Phone B** is the "hero" phone with YardSwaps on it. One person can do it alone with a cheap phone clamp or by leaning Phone A on something.
- One idle machine that is really for sale or really sitting: generator, compressor, pressure washer, trailer, mower, welder. Something a buyer recognises from across the yard.
- Daylight with the sun behind the camera. Late afternoon looks best.

## Before you roll (1 minute)
- Phone A: Settings > Camera > Record Video > **4K at 30 fps**. Hold it **upright (vertical)** for every shot. Lock focus and exposure by long-pressing on the machine (AE/AF LOCK).
- Phone B: turn on Do Not Disturb, full battery icon, brightness all the way up (so its screen reads on camera), Control Center > add **Screen Recording**.
- Out of frame: faces, license plates, street signs, house numbers, dealer banners, other people's logos. Hands, a glove or a jacket sleeve are all we show.
- Wipe Phone B's screen. Fingerprints catch the sun.

## The shots (record each 2 to 3 times, about 5 seconds each)
| # | Shot | How | Becomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The machine, alone** | Phone A at chest height, 3 to 4 steps back, whole machine in frame with room above and below. Hold still for 5 s, then one slow step forward. | Opener under "Equipment sitting idle?" |
| 2 | **Phone comes up** | Over the shoulder of whoever holds Phone B. Phone B rises into frame and frames the machine; the machine shows on Phone B's screen. Keep Phone A focused on Phone B's screen. | "Snap one photo." |
| 3 | **The tap** | Close on Phone B in a hand, screen readable: tap to focus on the machine, then the shutter. Hold 1 s after the tap. | The shutter beat (we add the sound) |
| 4 | **Screen recording on Phone B** (most important) | Start Screen Recording on Phone B. Open YardSwaps > Sell > pick the photo you just took. Let the AI fill the title, category and condition. Scroll once, tap Use price. Tap **Draft** at the end, not Publish, unless the machine is really for sale. Stop the recording. | The whole app part: real app, real photo, real AI result |
| 5 | **Hand puts the phone away** | Phone A wide again: Phone B lowers out of frame, machine stays in the shot. | Optional cutaway before the end card |
| 6 | **Detail** | Phone A close on the data plate or label of the same machine, steady, 3 s. | Optional: "Snap the plate" beat |

## How it gets cut (so you know what matters)
1 (2.0 s) > 2 (1.5 s) > 3 (0.8 s, shutter sound on the tap) > match cut into the screen recording at the moment the photo lands (about 7 s, sped up the same way as our Shorts) > end card (3.5 s). Same captions and end card as the camera-screen version. The item in shots 1 to 3 is the item in the screen recording, or the clip doesn't ship.
