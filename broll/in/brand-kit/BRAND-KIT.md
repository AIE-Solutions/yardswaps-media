# YardSwaps brand kit for Runway (Ads builder, Brand settings)

Folder: `assets/runway/ad-kit/brand/` (also in the media repo at `broll/in/brand-kit/`, branch claude/project-thread-673c79).

## Fields to paste into Runway

**Brand name:** YardSwaps
**Website:** yardswaps.com
**Brand description (paste as is):**
YardSwaps is a free marketplace app where businesses and tradespeople sell used equipment: fleet trucks, trailers, generators, pressure washers, compressors, welding and machining tools, forklifts, aerial lifts, HVAC, restaurant and refrigeration gear, warehouse and office equipment, and parts. Listing is free, there is no cut of the sale, and the app writes the listing from the seller's photos. Buyers message sellers directly in the app. Available on the App Store and Google Play, nationwide.

**Tone:** plain, concrete, calm, quietly funny. Documentary, not hype. No slogans, no exclamation marks, no promises of views or sales.

**Lines Runway may use on screen (pick one or two):**
- Free to list. No cut of the sale.
- Idle gear is cash sitting still.
- Every shop has one. List it free.
- Snap it. List it. Done.
- yardswaps.com

**Audience:** owners and crews at small contractors, shops, restaurants, farms, fleets and warehouses with gear sitting unused.

## Files

- `logo/yardswaps-logo-1024.png`, `logo/yardswaps-logo-512.png`: app icon (use as the logo).
- `wordmark/yardswaps-wordmark-on-light.png` (slate + blue, transparent), `yardswaps-wordmark-on-dark.png` (white + blue on slate deep), `yardswaps-wordmark-white.png`, `yardswaps-wordmark-slate.png`, plus the SVG sources.
- `colors/yardswaps-colors.png`: swatch sheet.
- `app-screens/01..06`: real app screenshots (home, listing detail, chat, AI scanner on the sell flow, search, account). Use these as the brand's product images if Runway asks for the product. Never let Runway redraw an app screen; the real screen is composited back before anything ships.
- Product photos for single-item ads: `../product-photos/` and `../range-shop/` (real listings, equipment only, no faces).

## Rules every Runway ad follows

- Machines never drive or dig; show idle, unused gear, app as the payoff at the end.
- No faces in real user photos; generated people are allowed in scenes.
- No made-up text or logos on equipment.
- End on a light-gray card: Free to list. No cut of the sale. yardswaps.com
- Turn on the platform's AI-generated label when posting.
