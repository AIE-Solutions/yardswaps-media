# Motion video captions (Oct 11, updated Oct 31)

Current captions for the 9 motion videos and the sponsor spot. Same text as the preview page Copy buttons. Instagram and TikTok say Link in bio because links do not click there.

App line (Alan, Oct 27; short form Oct 27 because Shorts and TikTok captions are not clickable): TikTok captions end with "List it free: yardswaps.com/t (link in bio). Get the app: search YardSwaps on the App Store or Google Play." and YouTube Shorts captions with "List it free: yardswaps.com/y (link in our channel profile). Get the app: search YardSwaps on the App Store or Google Play." (short links in /mnt/project-files/yardswaps-bio-short-links.md). Long-form YouTube videos (how-to, compilation, commercial) keep the full clickable line instead: "Get the app: iPhone https://apps.apple.com/app/id6765955041 | Android https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yardswaps.app | yardswaps.com/y". Facebook and Instagram stay as they are. YouTube hashtags no longer use #shorts (Oct 26 report).

Comment prompt (Oct 27, thread "Boost engagement with platform-specific tactics"): every caption on every platform carries one question for equipment owners, so viewers have something easy to answer. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok put it just before the link line; YouTube puts it right after the one-line summary; LinkedIn puts it just before the links block. New videos get a new question in the same style (seller-first, answerable in a few words, no yes/no). The question bank and the reasons are in ENGAGEMENT-PLAYBOOK.md.
